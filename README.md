# Framekeep

**Time-lapse camera for long shots: every photo is saved, even after a crash.**

Framekeep records time-lapses that run for hours and never lose the shot: every photo is saved on your phone the moment it is taken.

## What it does

- Takes one photo at the interval you choose, for as long as you need.
- Keeps going with the screen off. A notification shows the photo count and a Stop button.
- Plays your time-lapse inside the app, with a scrubber to reach any photo.
- Exports an MP4 video to your Movies folder, ready to share.

## Why it is different

Many time-lapse apps only write the video at the end, so a long shot can vanish if something goes wrong. Framekeep saves each photo as it is taken. If the app is closed or crashes, the session comes back at the next launch marked Recovered, with every photo taken until then, and it exports like any other.

## Free

- Six intervals, from 1 second to 1 minute
- Sessions up to 2 hours
- Recovery of interrupted sessions
- Tap to focus and a start timer up to 15 minutes
- In-app preview, and export up to 1080p at 30 fps

## Pro, a one-time unlock

- No time limit: record for days
- Exposure, focus and white balance lock, so the light stays steady from the first photo to the last
- Manual ISO, shutter speed, focus distance and white balance presets, wherever your camera allows them
- Day to night smoothing: auto exposure that moves a third of a stop at most per photo, for sunsets without jumps
- Deflicker at export, to smooth out brightness jumps
- Trim before export
- 4K export when your camera captures it, at 24, 30 or 60 fps
- Any interval from 1 second to 1 hour, to the second

One payment. No subscription, no ads.

## Private by design

No account and no sign-in. Your photos and videos stay on your phone and are never uploaded. The app never records sound and needs no access to your files.

Made for sunsets, drifting clouds, a plant growing, a city at night, a 3D print or a building site.
